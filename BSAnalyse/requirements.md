# BikeStore Analytics — Business User Requirements

**Requested by:** BikeStore Management Team
**Prepared by:** Analytics Team
**Date:** 2026-05-21
**Status:** Approved

---

## Business Background

BikeStore operates three retail stores selling bicycles, components, and accessories across
multiple brands and categories. The management team currently relies on manual reports and
spreadsheets to track performance, which makes it difficult to spot trends, compare stores,
or react quickly to stock issues.

The goal of this project is to provide a set of interactive dashboards that give store
managers and executives a clear, up-to-date picture of sales performance, stock health,
and team effectiveness — without needing to query the database themselves.

---

## Stakeholders

| Role | Primary Interest |
|---|---|
| **Executive / Owner** | Overall revenue, growth trends, which stores are performing |
| **Store Manager** | Daily sales, staff performance, which products are selling |
| **Inventory Manager** | Stock levels, low-stock alerts, restock priorities |
| **Sales Staff** | Their own order numbers and revenue contribution |

---

## Business Questions to Answer

### Sales
- How much revenue have we generated this month / quarter / year?
- Is revenue growing or declining compared to the same period last year?
- Which store is generating the most sales?
- Which products and categories sell the most?
- How many orders are completed vs. still pending or rejected?
- What percentage of orders have actually been shipped to customers?

### Inventory
- How much stock do we currently hold across all stores?
- What is the total estimated retail value of our current inventory?
- Which products are running dangerously low and need restocking urgently?
- Which store has the highest stock levels?
- Is our total inventory going up or down over time?
- Which brands and categories make up the bulk of our inventory value?

### Product & Staff Performance
- Which brands are our biggest revenue contributors?
- Are newer model-year bikes outselling older ones?
- Which staff members are closing the most sales?
- Who are our most valuable repeat customers?
- Which product categories are receiving the heaviest discounts?
- Is our average discount rate increasing over time?
